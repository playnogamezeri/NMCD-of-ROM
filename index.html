The project

A universe to explore

Nu Miracles of ROM is an expanding mythology and worldbuilding project. Its archive brings together divine beings, their domains and relationships, the five societies, story collections, and systems for exploring ideas.

The name ROM is used without an expanded phrase. Its meaning is not to be filled in by guesswork.
